Rhythm Monster

A fast-paced, high-performance rhythm game built with Godot 4.6. 
Battle the music by hitting the notes on time using your keyboard!


It is a 2D music notes game with falling colored notes, emmits light with the same color when you hit it....stay alive to reach the hieghst score.

How to Play

 Use the D, F, J, K keys to hit the notes as they cross the target line.
 
 Keep your combo high to maximize your score!
 
 Press *R* to restart if you miss too many notes.

calculating the score:
every note you touch perfectly gives you 100 point

for every streak (touching two or more notes in a row) you gain a combo 

keep your streak to reach the highest combo score
